# Meal Planner — Showcase

The most important screens, to show what Meal Planner does (for the gallery on hanstandt.be). Sample data only: invented meals and shopping lists, no family data.

One folder per screen language: `EN\`, `FR\`, `NL\`, `DE\`. Each screen as `.png` (full size, 780 px wide) and `.webp` (640 px, 23–36 KB, for the website). `00_Overview.png` = all screens on one picture.

Made by `make_showcase.py` from the screenshots of `manual/figures/shoot.mjs` (Meal Planner v2.3.0, phone size, light mode). To refresh after an app change: run `node manual/figures/shoot.mjs <lang>` for each language, then `py Showcase/make_showcase.py`.

## English

| No. | English |
|---|---|
| 01 | The week at a glance: breakfast, lunch and dinner for every day; busy days are marked Quick. |
| 02 | The AI chef suggests a whole week in one tap, and asks before it replaces meals you planned. |
| 03 | One shopping list per store, built by the AI from the week's meals; tick items off in the shop. |
| 04 | Choose your AI: Claude, ChatGPT or Grok, with your own API key, stored on the phone only. |
| 05 | The chef welcomes you; a PIN you choose keeps the family's planner private. |
