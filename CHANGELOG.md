# Changelog — Meal Planner

Newest version at the top.

## v2.3.0 — 12 October 2026

- **Choose your AI**: Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Grok (xAI), in the menu. Paste the API key of
  the service you choose; each key is stored on the phone only and never put in a backup.
- The Claude key hint now points to platform.claude.com/settings/keys (the old console address redirects there).
- Lock screen: the chef stays visible on short screens.
- **User manual v2.0** in English, French, Dutch and German, with screenshots in each language. It explains
  every step, also for people who are not technical: putting the app online with Netlify (Appendix B) and
  getting an API key for Claude, ChatGPT or Grok (Appendix C).
- Showcase: five screens per language for the website gallery.
- The download on hanstandt.be now holds only the app and its source code (`app\index.html`), with the
  licence, this changelog and a README. The manuals are separate downloads on the same page.

## v2.2.0 — 4 October 2026

The first version shared on hanstandt.be.

- Week planner: breakfast, lunch and dinner for every day, with notes and photos.
- Quick days: the AI chef suggests meals ready in 20 minutes or less.
- AI chef: a full week of meals in one tap; it asks before it replaces meals you already planned.
- Photo to shopping list: photograph a dish, the AI names it, one more tap puts its ingredients on the list.
- Shopping list per store (Delhaize, Lidl, Aldi, Massen and Other), with an AI list from the week's meals.
- Four languages: English, French, Dutch and German, including the AI's answers.
- Phone and tablet layout, light and dark mode, installable on the home screen.
- Backup of everything, photos included, to one file; a 4-digit PIN keeps the app private on a shared phone.
